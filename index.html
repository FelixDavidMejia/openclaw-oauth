OpenClaw Gmail Monitor

OpenClaw Gmail Monitor is a privately operated integration that helps its owner monitor their Gmail account and identify messages requiring attention.

Purpose

The integration uses Google OAuth authorization to access the owner's Gmail account for personal email monitoring and notifications. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept registrations from other users.

Access and control

Google account access requires the account owner's consent. The owner can revoke access at any time through their Google Account security settings.

For information about data handling, see the Privacy Policy.

Independent personal integration. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or the OpenClaw project.
