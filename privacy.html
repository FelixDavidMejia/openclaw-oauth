Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

OpenClaw Gmail Monitor is a personal, self-hosted integration operated solely for its owner's use. It is not available for public registration.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, the integration may access Gmail message metadata and message content as permitted by the OAuth scopes granted. The exact permissions are determined by the application's OAuth configuration.

How information is used

Gmail data is used to monitor the owner's mailbox, identify relevant messages, and generate personal summaries or notifications. Data is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.

Storage and service providers

The integration runs in an environment controlled by its owner. OAuth credentials and any locally retained monitoring data are managed in that environment. Depending on the configured monitoring workflow, selected message information may be processed by external AI or notification service providers. Such processing should be reviewed against the actual configuration before this policy is published.

Data sharing

The integration is not offered to other users and does not intentionally disclose Gmail data publicly. Any transfers to configured service providers are solely for operating the owner's monitoring workflow.

Retention and deletion

Any locally stored credentials, logs, or monitoring outputs are managed by the owner and can be deleted from the self-hosted environment. The owner may revoke Google access through their Google Account settings.

Security

Access is limited to the owner-controlled environment. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For questions about this personal integration, contact its owner through the contact address listed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's functionality or data practices change.

Independent personal integration. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or the OpenClaw project.
